## 🌟 Project Overview
This repository contains a production-grade, highly interactive **Financial Performance & Profit and Loss (P&L) Statement Dashboard** developed for a real-world corporate client. The solution bridges raw multi-channel financial datasets and executive-level decision making, providing C-suite stakeholders with real-time visibility into revenue streams, cost structures, cash flow, and bottom-line profitability.

## 💡 Key Financial Highlights & Business Metrics
* 💰 **Total Top-Line Revenue:** **€17.77M** across diversified revenue channels.
* 🛍️ **Product Sales:** **€11.77M** (accounting for 66.2% of total revenue streams).
* 💼 **Consulting Services:** **€6.00M** (accounting for 33.8% of total revenue streams).
* 📊 **Gross Profit:** **€10.59M** maintaining a robust **59.6% Gross Margin**.
* ⚙️ **Operating Profit (EBIT):** **€6.02M** yielding a strong **33.9% EBIT Margin**.
* 🎯 **Net Profit:** **€5.50M** solid bottom-line retention (**30.9% Net Margin**).
* 📉 **Cost Center Optimization:** Identified Raw Materials (**€6.45M**) as **89.9%** of total COGS (**€7.18M**), highlighting strategic vendor renegotiation targets.
* 📈 **Operating Expenses (Opex):** **€4.57M** tracked thoroughly across Consulting overhead (**€2.62M**) and R&D investments (**€1.43M**).
* 🔄 **Transactional Scale:** Processed over **10,948+ transactions** across **107 unique enterprise customers**, tracking total customer revenue volume of **$26.6M** with deep credit/debit cash flow metrics.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Technical Implementation
* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema architecture with automated relationship mapping and snowflaked dimensional hierarchies.
* **Data Cleansing & Audit:** Advanced data cleaning pipelines (`Cleaning Transactions.xlsx`, `Cleaning Balance Sheet by Class.xlsx`) and audit documentation (`Financial_Insights_Report.pdf`, `Financial_Data_Reconstruction_Report.pdf`).
* **DAX Formulas & Time Intelligence:** Custom measures for dynamic profit margins, matrix financial statements, and multi-parameter slicer filtering (Quarter, Month, Product Line, Class, and Days Overdue).
